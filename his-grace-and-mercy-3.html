Interdenominational, and open to everyone

We're a prayer ministry first — a place where intercessors, choir members, Bible study groups, and Sunday worshippers all have a home, regardless of the denomination you grew up in.

If you've never joined a prayer meeting before, Thursday's Hour of Grace and Mercy or Sunday service are both easy places to start.
